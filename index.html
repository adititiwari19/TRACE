Feature Recovery for Object Understanding After Irreversible Fire Damage

Understanding what objects were before physical destruction. TRACE benchmarks visual recognition across progressive post-fire damage and introduces feature recovery to help AI systems identify severely degraded objects.

21.4K Synthetic post-fire scenes
499 Object identities
5 Degradation levels
